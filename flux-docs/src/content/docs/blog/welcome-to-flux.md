Welcome to the official blog for **Flux**!

## What is Flux?

Flux — a write-once UI language for native iOS and Android. Write .flux, hot-reload on device over binary patches, and ship SwiftUI + Jetpack Compose.

## What to expect here

This blog is the central hub for everything related to Flux. We will be publishing:

- **Deep dives** into the architecture, design decisions, and inner workings of the project.
- **Tutorials and how-to guides** to help you get up and running quickly.
- **Release notes** and feature announcements as the project evolves.

Whether you are just discovering Flux or have been using it for a while, we are glad you are here. Stay tuned for our first official deep dive!
